IMPLEMENTATION CHOICES

A few details
made explicit.

The product specification and component layout are now locked after two independent static reviews. Old-tool retirement is approved. The defaults below define the build; actual application and wallet verification are still required.

P1 · Project addresses

Use /projects/adawatch, /projects/adamatic, /projects/uplc-link and /projects/cardano-dev-skills.

P2 · Old tools — retirement approved

The excluded dashboard and old Summit, Kreate, Vitevis and WMTx tools will retire with explicit 410 responses, including their verification APIs. Compatibility redirects: staking-assessment to Pool and the old UPLC path to its overview.

P3 · Type & motion

Keep the reviewed Poppins/serif treatment; restrained short transitions, no automatic orbit/parallax and full reduced-motion support.

P4 · Upcoming titles

Keep the remembered titles: From stake pool operator to Cardano developer; A real debugging walkthrough using uplc.link; Inside AdaMatic. No article links until a real article is ready.

P5 · Pool identity

Independent chain-data and hash-matched metadata checks corroborate the configured EASY1 target. Recheck at release; staking also requires a current pool-eligibility check.

P6 · Data freshness

Cache verified pool facts for up to five minutes; show unavailable when data cannot be refreshed within the accepted age. No sample figures.

P7 · Tracking

Add no new analytics platform in the initial launch. Later marketing measurement can be designed explicitly without wallet identifiers.
