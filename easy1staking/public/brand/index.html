04 / A FEW SIMPLE RULES

Keep it recognisable.

Identity source ↓
01

Give the E1 room.

Keep at least one stroke's width of clear space around the mark. Use the symbol on its own when the wordmark would be too small to read.

02

Match the background.

Use the light wordmark on dark surfaces and the navy wordmark on light ones. Use solid black or white when colour isn't appropriate.

03

Keep the shape intact.

Scale proportionally. Keep the E spine, three arms and diagonal cut together. Leave the gradient and letter spacing as supplied.

04

Choose the right file.

SVG stays sharp at any size and includes outlined lettering. PNG is ready for social uploads. The logo library is transparent; social assets include their backgrounds.
