EASY1STAKING BRAND KIT — CONNECTED SOFT

Open index.html in a browser to preview and download the assets.
The page also works at /brand/index.html when served by the existing website.

LOGOS
E1 symbol, horizontal logo, stacked logo and wordmark-only versions.
Each has colour/light lettering, colour/navy lettering, solid black and solid
white variants. All logo-library backgrounds are transparent.

SOCIAL
1024 x 1024 profile images (colour dark/light, black on white, white on black).
1500 x 500 X headers, 1584 x 396 LinkedIn PERSONAL profile covers, and
1200 x 630 share cards in dark and light versions. These have solid backgrounds.
The avatar preview is circular; the actual downloaded file remains square.
Check the platform's cropping preview before saving. The LinkedIn cover is
for personal profiles, not a LinkedIn company-page cover.

ICONS
16, 32, 48, 180, 192 and 512 px app/favicon PNGs, with SVG counterparts.

SOURCE
source/identity.json holds the E1 paths and palette.
source/wordmark.json holds the outlined easy1staking lettering.
Lettering is based on Poppins SemiBold under the SIL Open Font License.
The typeface source and its licence are included. SVGs need no installed font.
The geometric master was rebuilt from Giovanni's selected Connected Soft
concept; it is vector artwork, not an enlarged/cropped AI concept sheet.

REBUILD IN THE REPOSITORY
node scripts/build-brand-media.cjs
python3 scripts/package-brand-media.py
The SVG/PNG generator uses the project's installed sharp dependency.
